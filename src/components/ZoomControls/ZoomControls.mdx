# ZoomControls

Zoom out, the current percent with a menu of choices, and zoom in.

<Canvas of={Stories.StepsAndAMenu} />

## When to use

- **Beside a zoomable view**, such as a [DocumentViewport](?path=/docs/components-documentviewport--docs), so zoom does not depend on a wheel or a pinch.

## Usage

```tsx
<ZoomControls zoom={zoom} percent={scale * 100} onZoomChange={setZoom} />
```

Pass the percent the view came to (DocumentViewport's `onScaleChange` times 100): fit modes depend on the viewport, so only the view knows it.

## Behaviour and accessibility

- **Role:** a toolbar named "Zoom": one tab stop; Left and Right move between zoom out, the menu and zoom in.
- **Menu:** fit width, fit page and preset percents as a radio set; the current one is checked, and choosing closes the menu.
- **Limits:** zoom in stops at 800% and zoom out at 25% (the buttons disable).
- **Touch:** 44px hit areas on touch screens.
