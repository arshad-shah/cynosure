# ZoomControls

Zoom out, the current percent with a menu of choices, and zoom in.

<Canvas of={Stories.StepsAndAMenu} />

## When to use

- **Beside a zoomable view**, such as a [DocumentViewport](?path=/docs/components-structure-documentviewport--docs) or a [PanZoomCanvas](?path=/docs/components-structure-panzoomcanvas--docs), so zoom does not depend on a wheel or a pinch.

## Usage

```tsx
<ZoomControls zoom={zoom} percent={scale * 100} onZoomChange={setZoom} />
```

Pass the percent the view came to (DocumentViewport's `onScaleChange` times 100): fit modes depend on the viewport, so only the view knows it.

<Canvas of={Stories.DrivesADocumentViewport} />

### A canvas

A canvas has no pages to fit: leave out the document fit modes with `fits={[]}`, add `buttons={["fit", "actual-size"]}` (Fit to view reports `{ kind: "fit-page" }`, the whole content; Actual size reports 100%), and give it the canvas's own `min`, `max` and `steps`. Leave out `zoom`: the preset equal to the percent is marked. A PanZoomCanvas's handle applies every choice with `zoom(setting)`.

```tsx
<ZoomControls
  percent={view.scale * 100}
  min={50}
  max={200}
  steps={[50, 100, 200]}
  fits={[]}
  buttons={["fit", "actual-size"]}
  onZoomChange={(z) => canvas.current?.zoom(z)}
/>
```

## Behaviour and accessibility

- **Role:** a toolbar named "Zoom": one tab stop; Left and Right move between zoom out, the menu, zoom in and any `buttons`; Home and End jump to the first and last.
- **Menu:** the fit modes in `fits` (fit width and fit page unless given) and preset percents as a radio set; the current one is checked, and choosing closes the menu.
- **Buttons:** `fit` and `actual-size` follow zoom in, in the same toolbar (one tab stop). Actual size is disabled when 100% is outside the limits.
- **Steps:** zoom in and out go through `steps` (the document steps unless given).
- **Limits:** zoom in stops at 800% and zoom out at 25% (the buttons disable); give `min` and `max` for a view with other limits (a PanZoomCanvas at 20 to 300%). The menu offers only presets between them.
- **Touch:** 44px hit areas on touch screens.
