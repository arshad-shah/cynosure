# PanZoomCanvas

A surface to pan and zoom freely, for diagrams, maps and boards; your content, placed in canvas units.

<Canvas of={Stories.FitsItsContent} />

## When to use

- **Free two-dimensional content** bigger than the screen: diagrams, schemas, mind maps, boards.

**Use something else when:**
- the content is pages one under another: [DocumentViewport](?path=/docs/components-documentviewport--docs)
- it is a long list: [VirtualList](?path=/docs/components-virtuallist--docs)

## Usage

```tsx
<PanZoomCanvas ref={canvas} label="Schema" bounds={bounds} minimap renderMinimap={() => <Dots />}>
  {nodes.map((n) => <Node key={n.id} style={{ position: "absolute", left: n.x, top: n.y }} />)}
</PanZoomCanvas>
```

Place children absolutely in canvas units. Give `bounds` (the content's extent) to fit it on first show, for Shift+1 and `fit()`, and for the minimap. The ref has `fit`, `zoomTo` and `centreOn` for your own buttons; pair it with [ZoomControls](?path=/docs/components-zoomcontrols--docs) for a zoom toolbar.

## Behaviour and accessibility

- **Role:** a named region and tab stop. Zoom changes are announced ("Zoom 125%").
- **Pointer:** drag the empty background, or hold Space and drag anywhere, to pan; the wheel pans; Ctrl/Cmd+wheel zooms around the pointer. Presses on your content are left to it.
- **Touch:** one finger pans; two fingers pinch-zoom around their midpoint.

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows (Shift: further) | Pan |
| Ctrl/Cmd + = / - | Zoom in / out a step |
| Ctrl/Cmd + 0 | 100% |
| Shift + 1 | Fit the content |
| Space (held) + drag | Pan |
