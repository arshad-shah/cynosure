# TimeField

A time of day in a form: typed loosely or picked from columns that roll under a lens, and reported as 24-hour `HH:MM`.

<Canvas of={Stories.PickFromTheColumns} />

## When to use

- **A time of day** in a form or filter: an opening hour, a reminder, a schedule.

**Use something else when:**
- it is a date: [DateField](?path=/docs/components-datefield--docs)
- it is a duration or a count of minutes: [NumberField](?path=/docs/components-numberfield--docs)

## Usage

```tsx
import { TimeField } from "@arshad-shah/cynosure";

<TimeField label="Start time" value={start} onChange={setStart} min="09:00" max="17:30" step={15} />
```

The value is `HH:MM` (or `HH:MM:SS` with `seconds`) or `null`. `930`, `9:30 pm` and `12am` are understood; anything that is not a time is kept for correcting and explained.

<Canvas of={Stories.ExplainsWhatItCannotTake} />

### 12-hour display

`hourCycle={12}` shows `9:30 PM` and adds an AM/PM switch to the picker. The value, `min`, `max` and what a form receives stay 24-hour.

<Canvas of={Stories.TwelveHour} />

### Limits and the day track

The strip under the columns is the whole day: click or drag to jump to a time. The hours outside `min` and `max` are hatched, and their rows are struck through.

<Canvas of={Stories.TheDayTrack} />

### Typed only

`picker={false}` leaves a plain field; `dayTrack={false}` keeps the columns without the strip.

## Behaviour and accessibility

In the field:

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Enter | Finish typing |
| Up / Down | Step the part the caret is in: hours, minutes (by `step`), seconds, or AM/PM; Shift: ten times as far |

In the picker:

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Up / Down | Roll the focused column to the next allowed row; the time changes at once |
| Home / End | First and last allowed row |
| Left / Right | The column before or after |
| Digits | Seek in the column: `1` then `4` is 14 |
| A / P | Morning or afternoon, with `hourCycle={12}` |
| Enter | Close the picker and return to the field |
| Escape | Close the picker |
| Arrows, Page Up / Down on the day track | Move by `step`, or by an hour |

- **Errors** are the field's description and set `aria-invalid`.
- **Limits:** `min` and `max` stop the arrows and the day track; typed times outside them are explained; their rows in the picker are disabled.
- **The picker** is a dialog named by `labels.open`. Each column is a listbox named by `labels.hours`, `labels.minutes` or `labels.seconds`; the day track is a slider that reads out the time.
- **Scrolling** a column chooses the row it stops on. With reduced motion the columns jump instead of gliding.
- **Right to left:** hours, minutes and seconds keep their order.

## Touch

The field asks for the numeric keyboard (the full one with `hourCycle={12}`, to type AM and PM). In the picker, rows are at least 44px tall on coarse pointers and a column is rolled with a finger.

## Theming

The field follows TextField, the popup Popover, the day track Slider and the AM/PM switch SegmentedControl. The picker's own tokens:

| Token | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `--cy-time-field-row-height` | Height of a row in the columns (never below the touch hit area) |
| `--cy-time-field-column-width` | Width of one column |
| `--cy-time-field-lens-background` | Background of the band the chosen time sits in |
| `--cy-time-field-night` | Colour of the day track at midnight |
| `--cy-time-field-day` | Colour of the day track at midday |
