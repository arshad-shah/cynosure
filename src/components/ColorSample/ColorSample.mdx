# ColorSample

A colour shown as a surface, or text in one colour on another with its contrast ratio and the WCAG levels it meets.

<Canvas of={Stories.TextOnAColourWithItsContrast} />

## When to use

- **Showing a colour** that is not a choice: the base colour of a palette tool, a picked colour, a theme's surface.
- **Previewing text on a colour** with its contrast: a contrast checker, a theme editor.

**Use something else when:**
- people choose a colour: [ColorSwatches](?path=/docs/components-controls-colorswatches--docs) or [ColorField](?path=/docs/components-controls-colorfield--docs)
- several colours are shown in a row without a choice: `ColorSwatches readOnly`

## Usage

```tsx
import { ColorSample } from "@arshad-shah/cynosure";

<ColorSample color={base} label="Base colour" />
<ColorSample color={background} ink={text}>The quick brown fox</ColorSample>
```

<Canvas of={Stories.AColourAsASurface} />

## Contrast

With `ink`, the sample text is drawn in it and the contrast line shows the WCAG 2 ratio of the pair as seen (a translucent ink is blended onto the colour first). The ratio is cut, never rounded up, to two decimals, so a pair just under a level fails it. `largeText` judges the pair as large text: AA at 3:1 and AAA at 4.5:1.

`sampleContrast(ink, color)` gives the same ratio for your own checks.

## Behaviour and accessibility

- **Roles:** without an ink, an image named by `label` (default the colour). With an ink, a group named by `label` (default "#767676 on #ffffff"), holding the sample text and the contrast line.
- **Levels:** each is a Badge with a tick or a cross; its spoken text says "AA pass" or "AA fail" (`labels.pass`, `labels.fail`), so the verdict never rests on colour alone.
- **Not interactive:** it takes no focus and no presses.
- **Host CSS:** host rules for `div` and `span` do not change it (tested).

## Theming

Tokens: `--cy-color-sample-height`, `--cy-color-sample-radius` and `--cy-color-sample-gap`. The verdicts use the Badge tokens.
