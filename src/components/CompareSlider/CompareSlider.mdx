# CompareSlider

Two versions of the same thing, one over the other, split by a divider people move to compare them.

<Canvas of={Stories.BeforeAndAfterSplitByADivider} />

## When to use

- **Before and after** of an image edit, a redesign or a render, where the difference is in the detail.

**Use something else when:**
- people pick a number: [Slider](?path=/docs/components-slider--docs)
- two panes are both worked in: [SplitPane](?path=/docs/components-splitpane--docs)

## Usage

```tsx
import { CompareSlider } from "@arshad-shah/cynosure";

<CompareSlider before={<img src={original} alt="Original" />} after={<img src={edited} alt="Edited" />} />
```

`before` and `after` should be the same size. Control the divider with `value` and `onChange` (percent of the width showing `before`).

## Behaviour and accessibility

- **Role:** the divider is a slider named "Comparison", read as "40% before". Rename it and the tags with `labels`.
- **Pointer:** press anywhere on the picture to jump the divider there (and focus it), then drag; Escape mid-drag puts it back.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move 1%, Shift+arrows and Page Up/Down 10%, Home and End go to the ends.
- **Touch:** a sideways drag moves the divider at once and a tap jumps it; an upward or downward swipe scrolls the page. The divider's hit area is 44px on touch screens.
