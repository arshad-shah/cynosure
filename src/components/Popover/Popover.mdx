# Popover

A small floating panel attached to a button, for options and details that belong to that button: filters, view settings, a quick form. It sits in the browser's top layer, so no parent can clip it.

<Canvas of={Stories.OpensFocusesAndReturnsFocus} />

## When to use

- **A few related controls** that would clutter the page if always visible (filters, display options).
- **Extra detail** about one item, with links or buttons in it.

**Use something else when:**
- it is a short text hint with no controls: [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs)
- it is a list of commands: [Menu](?path=/docs/components-overlays-menu--docs)
- the person must finish or cancel before continuing: [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-overlays-dialog--docs)
- the content is long or is a whole task: a page or a [Sheet](?path=/docs/components-overlays-sheet--docs)

## Usage

```tsx
import { Button, Checkbox, Popover, Stack } from "@arshad-shah/cynosure";

<Popover label="Filters" trigger={(props) => <Button {...props}>Filters</Button>}>
  <Stack gap="4">
    <Checkbox label="Only errors" />
    <Button variant="solid" tone="accent">Apply</Button>
  </Stack>
</Popover>
```

`trigger` is a render prop: spread the props it receives onto your [Button](?path=/docs/components-controls-button--docs) or [IconButton](?path=/docs/components-controls-iconbutton--docs). They carry the ref, the click handler and the ARIA wiring (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `aria-haspopup`).

## Layers

Popovers nest, and Escape closes one layer at a time: an inner popover first, then the outer one. A [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs) shown inside a popover also closes before the popover does.

<Canvas of={Stories.NestedCloseOneAtATime} />

## Focus and width

`initialFocus` says where focus goes on open: the `first` control (default), the `panel` itself, or `none`. With `none`, focus stays where it was, so a hint can open beside a field while the person keeps typing; Escape and an outside press still close it, and Tab moves into it when the panel follows the field in the document (render the Popover right after its anchor; with a trigger this is automatic).

<Canvas of={Stories.InitialFocusNoneKeepsTheCaretWhileTyping} />

`width="trigger"` makes the panel at least as wide as its trigger or anchor element, lined up with it; `width="fixed"` gives it exactly the `size` preset however short its content is. The default, `content`, sizes it to its content up to the preset.

<Canvas of={Stories.WidthMatchesTheTrigger} />

## Alignment and gap

`align` lines the panel up with its trigger across the side it opens on: `start`, `center` or `end`, following the reading direction (beside the trigger, `start` is the top edge and `end` the bottom). Unset, the panel starts at the trigger's start edge above or below it, and is centred beside it. `offset` is the gap in pixels (default 6).

<Canvas of={Stories.AlignLinesThePanelUpWithTheTrigger} />

## At a point or beside an element

`anchor` places the panel against something other than its trigger: a point (`{ x, y }` in viewport pixels, such as where a canvas was clicked), any element, or a virtual anchor (anything with `getBoundingClientRect`). `trigger` is then optional; control the panel with `open`, and focus goes back to where it was when it closes.

```tsx
<Popover label="Add a note" anchor={at ?? undefined} open={at !== null} onOpenChange={(o) => !o && setAt(null)}>
  <TextField label="Note" />
</Popover>
```

## Behaviour and accessibility

| Input | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Click, tap, <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd> on the trigger | Opens or closes |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Closes the topmost layer and returns focus to its trigger |
| Pointer down outside | Closes it |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> with `modal` | Cycles through the panel's tab stops (a toolbar or radio group is one stop) |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> past the last control, without `modal` | Closes it; focus stays where Tab put it |

- **Focus:** on open, focus moves to the first control inside that Tab reaches, or to the panel itself when it has none, so screen readers land in it (`initialFocus` changes this). On close, focus returns to the trigger unless you already moved it somewhere else.
- **Focus leaving:** a non-modal panel closes when focus moves out of it to anything but its trigger or anchor (Tab past its last control, a click into another field), so it never hangs open over what has focus. Shift+Tab back to the trigger keeps it open. `closeOnFocusOut={false}` keeps it open until Escape, an outside press or the trigger closes it.
- **Anchor:** an anchor element counts as part of the popover, like the trigger: pressing it is not an outside press.
- **Role:** the panel is a dialog named by `label`. With `modal` it is `aria-modal` and keeps Tab inside.
- **Position:** the panel prefers `placement`, flips to the other side when there is no room, and stays inside the viewport. `start` and `end` follow the reading direction.
- **Shadow DOM:** works inside shadow roots (extension content scripts).
- **Motion:** a short fade and scale, removed under reduced motion.

## Theming

Tokens: `--cy-popover-padding`, `--cy-popover-radius` and `--cy-popover-width-{sm,md,lg}`.

```ts
createTheme({ components: { popover: { radius: "10px", widthMd: "360px" } } });
```
