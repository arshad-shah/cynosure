# Calendar

A month of days to pick one from, or to lay out a month's plans on.

<Canvas of={Stories.PickADay} />

## When to use

- **Choosing a day** where seeing the month helps (a booking, a due date).
- **A month view** of plans or events: `renderDay` puts your content in each day, `markDay` tints days and adds dots.

**Use something else when:**
- the date goes into a form field: [DateField](?path=/docs/components-datefield--docs), which types or picks
- people pick a time of day: [TimeField](?path=/docs/components-timefield--docs)

## Usage

```tsx
import { Calendar } from "@arshad-shah/cynosure";

<Calendar value={date} onChange={setDate} min="2026-01-01" isDateDisabled={isFullyBooked} />
```

Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings: no time and no time zone. Month and day names come from `Intl` in the `locale` you give (the browser's by default), and the week starts where that locale starts it.

## Limits and unavailable days

`min` and `max` stop focus and the month buttons. `isDateDisabled` marks days that can be focused (so people can hear about them) but not chosen.

<Canvas of={Stories.LimitsAndUnavailableDays} />

## A month of plans

`renderDay(date, state)` adds your content under each day's number; the grid then fills its container. `onMonthChange` tells you which month to load.

<Canvas of={Stories.AMonthOfPlans} />

With `renderDay` a day is at least `--cy-calendar-content-size` tall: 2.5 times the cell size by default, and 66px on touch screens (1.5 times the 44px target), so a six-week month is under 520px tall in a 390px-wide container. Set the token for taller or shorter days; a day never drops below the touch target.

## Marked days

`markDay(date, state)` marks a day without drawing it. Return any of:

- `tone`: tints the day softly with that tone. Days of other months are not tinted; the selected day keeps its fill and today its ring.
- `dots`: one small dot per entry under the number, each a tone or any CSS colour. Three at most are drawn; further entries are dropped, so put the count in `label`.
- `label`: words added to the day's name after its date, so the marks are not colour alone.

`state` is `{ selected, today, outside, disabled, weekend }`. `weekend` follows the locale where the browser knows its weekend, and is Saturday and Sunday otherwise.

```tsx
<Calendar
  markDay={(date, day) => ({
    tone: day.weekend ? "neutral" : undefined,
    dots: tasks[date]?.map((task) => task.colour),
    label: tasks[date] ? `${tasks[date].length} tasks` : undefined,
  })}
/>
```

## The month shown

Left alone, the calendar starts on `defaultMonth` (or the month of the value or focused date), moves itself, and follows a new `value` to its month.

Pass `month` (`YYYY-MM`, or any `YYYY-MM-DD` date of that month) to own it. Every way of leaving the month then calls `onMonthChange` with the first day of the month asked for, and the grid waits for the new `month`. If you do not move, focus stays on the day it was on. A new `value` in another month does not move a controlled month: set `month` too.

```tsx
const [month, setMonth] = useState("2026-11");

<Calendar month={month} onMonthChange={setMonth} />
```

<Canvas of={Stories.ControlledMonth} />

## Jumping to a month and year

`monthPicker` turns the heading into a button that opens a panel: a year to step through and its twelve months. Months and years outside `min` and `max` are disabled. It is off by default.

## Behaviour and accessibility

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Left / Right | Previous / next day (flipped in right-to-left text) |
| Up / Down | Same day of the previous / next week |
| Home / End | First / last day of the week |
| Page Up / Page Down | Same day of the previous / next month (Shift: year) |
| Enter, Space | Choose the focused day |

- **Roles:** a `grid` named by the month title, column headers with the full day name as `abbr`, and one Tab stop on the focused day.
- **Names:** each day reads as its full date, then any `markDay` label, then any `renderDay` content; today is `aria-current="date"`; unavailable days are `aria-disabled`.
- **Announcements:** the month title is a polite live region.
- **Focus:** the focused day is always a day of the month shown. When the month changes it is the same day number there (or the month's last day), kept within `min` and `max` where the month has such a day.
- **Month picker:** the heading button is named by the month and "Choose month and year" and opens a dialog. Focus moves into it; <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves through the year buttons and the months, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> chooses a month, closes the panel and puts focus back on the heading. <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes it and changes nothing. The month shown is `aria-current`.
- **Forced colours:** a tinted day has a dashed outline and dots take the text colour.
- **Labels:** `labels` replaces `previous`, `next`, `monthPicker`, `previousYear` and `nextYear`.

## Touch

Days are at least 44px on touch screens, also with `renderDay`: a grid in a container narrower than seven targets scrolls. Tap a day to choose it; the month buttons and the month picker's buttons have the same hit area.

## Theming

| Token | Sets |
| --- | --- |
| `--cy-calendar-cell-size` | A day's size (never below the hit area) |
| `--cy-calendar-selected-background` | Background of the selected day |
| `--cy-calendar-content-size` | With `renderDay`, the least height of a day (never below the hit area) |
| `--cy-calendar-tone-mix` | How much of a marked day's tone is mixed into its background (12%) |
| `--cy-calendar-dot-size` | Size of a day's dots |

The selected day uses the accent and its on-accent text colour.
