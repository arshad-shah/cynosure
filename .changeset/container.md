New `Container`: the page column. It centres its content, caps its width (`size`: `sm` 640px, `md` 768px, `lg` 1024px by default, `xl` 1280px, or `full` for no cap) and adds side gutters from a space token that never get narrower than a phone's safe area. `paddingBlock`, `center` and `as` (`div`, `main`, `section`, `article`) cover the rest, and the widths are tokens.
