New `Avatar`: a person, team or thing as a picture, falling back to initials worked out from the name (emoji and non-Latin names included) and then to an icon. It has five sizes, circle and square shapes, tones (with `tone="auto"` picking one from the name), a status dot that is spoken with the name, and `decorative` for when the name is written beside it.

New `AvatarGroup`: overlaps several avatars along the reading direction, gives them one size and sums up the rest as a "+3" avatar that names the hidden people.
