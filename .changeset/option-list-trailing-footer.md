OptionList: options take `trailing` content (a shortcut or a count at the row's end) and `dimmed`, and the list draws a `footer` action pinned under the options (`{ action, id, active, onActive, onRun }`). Select and Combobox options take `trailing` and `dimmed`, and both fields take a `footer` action (`{ label, icon?, onSelect }`) that the arrow keys reach after the last option.
