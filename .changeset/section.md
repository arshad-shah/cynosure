New `Section`: a full-width band of a page with a background (`variant`: `plain`, `surface`, `soft`, `accent`), padding above and below from a space token, and its content in a Container (`size`, or `contained={false}`). `as`, `id`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-label` and `role` are set on the root, so a Section can be a named region and an in-page link target.
