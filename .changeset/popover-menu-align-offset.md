Popover and Menu: `align` (`start`, `center` or `end`, following the reading direction; beside the trigger, `start` is the top edge and `end` the bottom) lines the panel up with its trigger across the side it opens on, and `offset` sets the gap in pixels (Popover's default stays 6; Menu's stays the `--cy-menu-offset` token). Unset, both keep today's placement.
