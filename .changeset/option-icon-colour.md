Icons in options can be coloured. `Select` and `Combobox` options and `Menu` and `ContextMenu` items take `iconTone` and `iconColor`, with the meaning of Icon's `tone` and `color`. Select keeps the colour in its trigger once the option is chosen. Disabled options show the icon muted, and icons without the new fields look as before.
