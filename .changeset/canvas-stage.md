CanvasStage (new): a host for a product's own drawing surface (a canvas an engine paints): a sized stage (`ratio`, or its parent's height), the surface stretched to fill it, a centred `overlay` slot for a picker or progress that lets presses around it through, and, with `onFiles`, a file drop target over the whole stage (`accept`, `multiple`, `onReject`, `labels.drop`).
