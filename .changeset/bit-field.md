BitField: the bits of a number (a `bigint`, so 64-bit words work) as a row of square pressed Buttons in a Toolbar, highest first and grouped by byte (`groupSize`), with one tab stop and the arrows between the bits; a press or Space flips a bit and `onChange` reports the new value. Groups and bits are named through `labels`.
