Tabs: `TabList overflow="menu"` puts the tabs that do not fit the list's width into a More menu after it, in order, with their icons and shortcuts; the selected tab always keeps its place in the row, and choosing a tab from the menu selects it. Widths are measured as the list and its tabs change size; arrow keys move through the tabs in the row. `Tab` gains `shortcut` (`aria-keyshortcuts`, and the hint in the menu), and `labels.more` names the button and menu (default "More").
