New ScrollArea: a box that fills its parent and scrolls, with `direction`, `follow`, `onScroll`, `onAtBottomChange`, an `overlay` that stays put (its root is an OverlayFrame), and a `handle` with `scrollToEnd` and `scrollIntoView`. With a `label` it is a named region and a tab stop while there is something to scroll.
