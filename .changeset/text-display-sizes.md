Text: display sizes `3xl`, `4xl` and `5xl` for one large figure (a countdown, a clock), set by the `--cy-text-size-3xl`, `-4xl` and `-5xl` tokens with a tighter line height (`--cy-text-display-line-height`). With `numeric` the digits keep their width as they tick.
