Dialog: `size="full"` fills the viewport edge to edge at every width (inside the safe areas, no radius, border or shadow, a fade without scaling), for a focus view of one pane or a page preview; it does not become a bottom sheet in a narrow container. `titleHidden` hides the title visually while it stays the dialog's accessible name and `h2`, leaving only the close button in the header. Sheet keeps its own `sm`, `md` and `lg` sizes.
