`Calendar` can have its month set from outside: `month` (controlled, `YYYY-MM` or any date of the month) and `defaultMonth`, with `onMonthChange` reporting the month asked for. `monthPicker` turns the month heading into a button that opens a panel to jump to any month and year within `min` and `max`. The focused day now always stays inside the month shown.
