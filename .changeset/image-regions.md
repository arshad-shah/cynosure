Image: `regions` outlines areas of the picture given in its own pixels (`{ x, y, width, height, label?, tone? }`, the `ImageRegion` type), such as where each code was found in a scan. They are drawn on the overlay's box, so they follow the picture at any size, `fit` and `position`; a `label` is a tag in the region's corner. Themed with `--cy-image-region-color` and `--cy-image-region-width`.
