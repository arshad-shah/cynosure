New `List`: a real `ul` or `ol` with drawn markers (`disc`, `number`, `check`, `icon`, `tile` or `none`), each item a label with an optional description. Numbered lists are ordered lists that count from `start` and suit how-to instructions; `gap`, `divided`, `size` and `tone` adjust the look, and markers follow the reading direction.
