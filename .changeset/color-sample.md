ColorSample (new): a colour shown as a surface, or text in one colour (`ink`) on another with its WCAG contrast ratio, cut to two decimals, and AA and AAA verdicts (`largeText` for the large-text thresholds). A translucent colour or ink is blended as seen. `sampleContrast(ink, color)` is exported.
