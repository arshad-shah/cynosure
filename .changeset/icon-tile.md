New `IconTile`: a Facet icon on a tinted square or circle, for features, settings sections, stats and list items. It has three sizes (the control heights, so it scales with the theme), the six tones, `soft`, `solid` and `outline` variants, and is decorative unless it has a `label`, which makes it an image with that name.
