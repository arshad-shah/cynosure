TitleBar: the title bar of an installed app that draws its own (frameless Electron windows, Window Controls Overlay). The whole bar drags the window while the controls inside stay clickable, and it takes `leading`, `trailing`, `leadingInset`, an `overlay` mode, `onHeightChange` and window `controls` (minimize, maximize or restore, close).
