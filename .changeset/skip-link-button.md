`SkipLink` is shown as an accent outline `Button` link, hidden by the shared `.cy-visually-hidden` until it has focus (still CSS only, so it works without JavaScript), and takes `contained` to show at the corner of the nearest positioned ancestor (AppShell sets it). Restyle the shown link through the `--cy-button-*` tokens; SkipLink's own tokens are `offset` and `radius`.
