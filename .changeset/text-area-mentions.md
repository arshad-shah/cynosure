TextArea: mentions. `trigger` (such as "@") and `suggestions` (a list, or a function of the query returning items or a promise) open the shared drop-down list at the start of a word; arrows move, Enter or Tab inserts the value and a space, Escape closes it, and `onMention` reports the choice. New `labels.suggestions` and `labels.loading`.
