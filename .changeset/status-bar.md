StatusBar and StatusBarSegment: the bar along the bottom of a desktop app, with start and end clusters of segments that show a tone, an icon or a status dot and monospace text, and as buttons open a Popover above them; arrow keys move between the clickable ones. `--cy-status-bar-soft-mix` sets the tint of soft segments, static or clickable.
