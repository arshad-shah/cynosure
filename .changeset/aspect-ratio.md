New `AspectRatio` component: a box that keeps a width-to-height ratio at any width, for a video, a map, a chart or an embed. `ratio` takes a number or a CSS ratio such as `"4/3"` (default `16 / 9`) and the height comes from CSS, so nothing is measured. Its children are stretched to the box, images and videos cover it, and `radius` rounds and clips the corners from the radius tokens.
