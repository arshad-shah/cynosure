RowActions: the buttons at the end of a row, hidden with a mouse until the row is hovered or holds focus or a menu of theirs is open, always shown on touch screens, and in the Tab order only on the row that holds the Tab stop. ListRow, Tree and ChatMessage render their actions with it, so they share one reveal rule (Tree's actions now fade by opacity like the others').
