Table: a column's `tone` tints its header and cells, and `tone: (row) => Tone | undefined` tints cell by cell for a heat grid; in markup, `TableCell` and `TableHeaderCell` take `tone`. The tone is mixed into the surface (`--cy-table-tint-mix`, 14%) with the text kept in the ink colour.
