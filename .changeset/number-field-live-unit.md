NumberField: `commit="input"` applies typed text as it is typed, whenever it reads as a number within `min` and `max` (Enter and leaving the field still clamp and rewrite it); `unit` shows text after the number ("px") that is read with the value but not typed (token `--cy-number-field-unit-color`); `decrementIcon` and `incrementIcon` take Facet icons for the steppers; `steppersFocusable` makes the steppers Tab stops, with their tooltips on focus.
