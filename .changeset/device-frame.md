New `DeviceFrame`: a phone, tablet or browser window drawn in CSS around a screenshot, a video or a live demo. The screen keeps its ratio at any width and clips its content to its corners, `orientation` turns a phone or tablet, and the browser bar shows an optional `url`. It is decoration by default and adds nothing to the accessibility tree; with `label` it is a named figure.
