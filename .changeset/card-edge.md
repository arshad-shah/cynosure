`Card` takes `edge`: a coloured edge on its inline-start side for a category or status. A tone name uses that tone's colour and any other string is a CSS colour. It follows the reading direction and the card's radius, works with every variant and on link and button cards, and its thickness is the new token `--cy-card-edge-width`. Pair it with text or a Badge, since colour alone carries no meaning for many people.
