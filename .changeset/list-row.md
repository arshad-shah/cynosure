New ListRow and ListRows: interactive list rows (notifications, activity, saved queries, connections) with a leading slot, a title and two meta lines, a trailing slot, actions shown on hover or focus (always on touch), and `active`, `unread`, `dimmed` and `tone` looks. The rows are buttons or links with one Tab stop and arrow keys, and Right moves into a row's actions.
