`Checkbox` has a checklist style: with `strikeWhenChecked`, a checked box strikes through and mutes its label and mutes the description, so a finished task reads as done. The muted label keeps 4.5:1 contrast in light and dark, the strike stays in forced colours, and the new tokens `--cy-checkbox-done-color` and `--cy-checkbox-strike-width` adjust it. Switch and RadioGroup are unchanged.
