New component: Pagination, for moving between the pages of a list or table. It is a navigation landmark of Buttons with previous and next, numbered pages with ellipses, optional first and last controls, a compact "Page 3 of 10" variant for phones, and real links through `getHref`.

The helpers `pageCount(total, pageSize)` and `pageRange(page, count, siblings, boundaries)` are exported with it.
