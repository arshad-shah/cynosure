New `Divider`: a line that separates content. It is horizontal by default and fills its container; `orientation="vertical"` stretches it to the height of a row such as an Inline or a footer of links. It can carry a short label in the line (`labelAlign` places it), be `dashed`, take a `strong` tone and add its own `spacing`. It is decorative by default; `decorative={false}` makes it a `role="separator"` named by its label.
