New `Text` component: one piece of text in the type scale, for labels, captions, lead paragraphs, helper lines and values. It has presets (`body`, `lead`, `caption`, `eyebrow`, `code`), `size`, `weight`, `tone` and `align` props that map to the font and colour tokens, one-line or multi-line `truncate`, and `numeric` for tabular figures. It renders a `span` by default or the element given in `as`, adds no margins, and is not changed by host CSS.
