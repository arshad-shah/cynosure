New `Checklist`: a named list of rows people add, tick, rename in place and remove, such as a task's subtasks. It works controlled (`items`) or uncontrolled (`defaultItems`), moves focus to a sensible control after every change, and has `maxItems`, `reorderable` (drag or keyboard, through SortableList), `disabled`, `readOnly`, two sizes and overridable labels.
