Ten new Facet icons. `deleteLeft` is the Backspace key (a left-pointing key with an x). Panel collapse and expand pairs, one per edge, each the existing panel outline with a chevron pointing in (close) or out (open): `panelLeftClose`, `panelLeftOpen`, `panelRightClose`, `panelRightOpen`, `panelTopClose`, `panelTopOpen`, `panelBottomClose`, `panelBottomOpen`. `fileRepair` is a page with a wrench, in the `file-*` family. Each has search words in `ICON_KEYWORDS` and motion on its moving part.
