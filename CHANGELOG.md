# @arshad-shah/cynosure

## 0.5.0

### Minor Changes

- b5558b8: Badge: leave out the text for a status dot alone in the tone's own colour: with a `label` it is an image named by it, without one it is decoration hidden from assistive technology.
- b5558b8: Button: `--cy-button-hover-bg` and `--cy-button-hover-ink` tokens set the fill and text colour on hover, for one button whose hover differs from its variant.
- b5558b8: Button: `--cy-button-square-width` sets the width of a square (icon-only) button such as an IconButton; it defaults to the height. TitleBar widens its window controls with it.
- b5558b8: Card: `title` and `description` put a header above the content (`headingLevel`, default 3), sharing its row with `actions`. New token `--cy-card-title-size`.
- b5558b8: Every palette has `seriesColors`, eight chart colours from the accent that read 3:1 on the page (`--cy-color-series-1` to `-8`), and `resolveColors` and `swiftChartTheme` give canvas engines and swift-chart concrete colours, without a chart dependency.
- b5558b8: ChatMessage is one message in an assistant chat: the person's at the end, the assistant's after an avatar, with a streaming caret, a danger tone, wide content and actions shown on hover or focus.
- b5558b8: CodeBlock: `actions` puts your own buttons in the header before the copy button, such as "Insert into editor".
- b5558b8: Combobox and CommandPalette mark the typed text in their option labels with HighlightedText (in CommandPalette, each typed word). Options are still named by their whole label.
- b5558b8: CommandPalette: a `filter(command, query)` prop replaces the built-in search with your own (a fuzzy matcher's `{ score, positions }` ranks the results and marks exactly those letters). Search results are now one flat list in rank order with each command's group shown as a small tag, as recent commands are; browsing without a query still lists commands under their group headings.
- b5558b8: ContextMenu: one menu can serve many targets (the rows of a list, the tabs of a tab list) through the render prop's `targetFor(key)`; `label` and `items` may be functions of the key, and `onSelect` and `onOpenChange` receive it. Tabs and Tree use it instead of their own copies.
- b5558b8: DataGrid is a read-only grid for large results: rows and columns render only where they are in view (a hundred thousand rows by fifty columns stay quick), under a sticky header, with the APG grid keyboard.
- b5558b8: Disclosure: `edge` draws a coloured inline-start edge in a tone or any colour, as Card's does, and `trailing` puts a Badge or a Button at the end of the summary row, outside the trigger button. New token `--cy-disclosure-edge-width`.
- b5558b8: ErrorBoundary: a new component that catches errors thrown while its children render and shows a fallback in their place (a danger Notice with "Try again" by default, or your own `fallback(error, retry)`), with `resetKey`, `onError` and `label`.
- b5558b8: New Field tokens `--cy-field-control-border`, `--cy-field-control-bg`, `--cy-field-control-radius` and `--cy-field-control-sunken-bg` restyle the box of every field at once: TextField (and the fields built on it) and HotkeyField read them under their own tokens.
- b5558b8: Field and every control built on it (TextField, Select, Combobox, TagInput, PathField, NumberField, DateField, TimeField, ColorField, TextArea, HotkeyField; the `FieldLayoutProps` type): `layout="split"` puts the label and description at the start and the control at the end of one row (a settings row), moving the control under the text when narrow, and `width` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`, `full`) lines the controls of a column up. New tokens `--cy-field-control-width-sm`, `-md`, `-lg` and `--cy-field-split-text-min`.
- b5558b8: FileDrop: `onPick` replaces the browser's file dialog with an app's own picker, as PathField's `onPick` (the button is busy until its promise settles; chosen files are checked and arrive in `onFiles`, chosen paths in `onPaths`), and a controlled `value` lists the chosen files or paths in the zone, each with a remove button that calls `onRemove(item, index)`. New token `--cy-file-drop-file-background`.
- b5558b8: PanZoomCanvas takes a `handle` prop, as ScrollArea and VirtualList do, and its handle has `centerOn` (American spelling, as the rest of the API). `ref` still gives the handle and `centreOn` still works; both are kept for existing code. VirtualList's `scrollToIndex(index, { align })` takes an options object, as ScrollArea's `scrollIntoView` does; the earlier `scrollToIndex(index, "center")` form still works.
- b5558b8: New HighlightedText: text with the parts that matched a search marked, from a fuzzy matcher's `positions` or every occurrence of a `query` (one term or several), ignoring case. It reads as one piece of text to screen readers and marks matches with the system colours in forced-colours mode. `textMatches(text, query)` filters by the same rule, and Combobox, TagInput, TextArea's mentions, Tree and CommandPalette use it, so what a list keeps and what it marks always agree.
- b5558b8: HotkeyField records a keyboard shortcut for a keybindings page, written as `useHotkey` reads it; `hotkeyFromEvent` turns a key press into that string, and `useHotkey` now also accepts `space` as a key name.
- b5558b8: Icons: a new entry, `@arshad-shah/cynosure/icons/by-name`, exports `iconByName(name)` and the `icons` record of every Facet icon by its name, for names that arrive as strings (plugin manifests). It brings every icon with it, so the main entry and `@arshad-shah/cynosure/icons` never import it and stay tree-shaken.
- b5558b8: Facet icons: 24 new icons, each with search words and a motion: file-question, file-search, file-plus, window-maximize, window-restore, panel-right, scissors, text-select, clipboard-paste, function (exported as `functionIcon`), rows, square-plus, cable, boxes, shield-question, gauge, check-check, power-off, loader, party-popper, rocket, hourglass, languages and monitor-down.
- b5558b8: Stack, Inline and Grid take `grow`, `shrink`, `fill`, `minSize="0"` and `overflow`, for app shells whose middle takes the free space and scrolls; GridItem takes `overflow`.
- b5558b8: New ListRow and ListRows: interactive list rows (notifications, activity, saved queries, connections) with a leading slot, a title and two meta lines, a trailing slot, actions shown on hover or focus (always on touch), and `active`, `unread`, `dimmed` and `tone` looks. The rows are buttons or links with one Tab stop and arrow keys, and Right moves into a row's actions.
- b5558b8: New `LiveRegion` foundation and `useLiveMessage` hook: the one hidden live region every component announces through, so the same message twice is spoken twice. FileDrop, TagInput, Tabs, VirtualList and SortableList now announce a repeated message again (a second "1 file added"), and every component's hidden region carries `aria-live` and `aria-atomic`.
- b5558b8: LoadingDots: `variant="spinner"` draws the `loader` icon turning, icon-sized and in the current colour, with the same `size`, `tone`, `color`, `speed` and `label`; reduced motion leaves it still.
- b5558b8: Menu: `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`, so an app can open a menu from elsewhere, such as a keyboard shortcut.
- b5558b8: Menu: `--cy-menu-offset` sets the gap between a menu and its trigger (default `--cy-space-2`), and Menubar's menus read it too instead of a fixed 4px.
- b5558b8: Menu, ContextMenu and Menubar: an item with `items` opens a submenu beside it, nested to any depth, following the APG menu keys (Right/Left, RTL aware), opening on hover after a short delay that tolerates diagonal travel, and closing the whole tree on a choice; `onSelect` receives values from any level.
- b5558b8: Menu, ContextMenu and Menubar: a section's `label` is optional; a section without one is set apart by separator lines instead of a named group.
- b5558b8: Menubar: a new APG menubar for a desktop app's application menu, a row of titles each opening a Menu (`label`, `menus: { label, items }[]`, `onSelect`, `size`). One menu is open at a time; Left and Right, hovering and tapping move between menus.
- b5558b8: Named themes: `createTheme({ mode })` fixes a theme to light or dark (the `ThemeMode` type) so the colour-mode switch does not flip it, and `defineTheme` and `createThemeRegistry` check plain-JSON theme definitions (plugin themes) at runtime, rejecting bad input or unreadable text with `CynosureThemeError`. `useCynosureTheme()` returns the theme, palette and resolved colours where it is called, for canvas and chart code.
- b5558b8: Notice: `variant="solid"` paints the tone's solid colour with the text colour made for it, and `size="sm"` tightens the padding and keeps the title and message on one line when they fit. New token `--cy-notice-padding-sm`.
- b5558b8: OptionList: options take `trailing` content (a shortcut or a count at the row's end) and `dimmed`, and the list draws a `footer` action pinned under the options (`{ action, id, active, onActive, onRun }`). Select and Combobox options take `trailing` and `dimmed`, and both fields take a `footer` action (`{ label, icon?, onSelect }`) that the arrow keys reach after the last option.
- b5558b8: New OverlayFrame: a frame whose content fills it, with an `overlay` pinned at the bottom centre that stays put while the content scrolls (tokens `--cy-overlay-frame-offset` and `--cy-overlay-frame-layer`). ScrollArea and VirtualList draw their `overlay` with it.
- b5558b8: PanZoomCanvas takes `claimPress`: asked about each press with its point in canvas units, it can claim the press to drag a node (children or painted content) instead of panning, with the drag reported in canvas units.
- b5558b8: PanZoomCanvas's `renderScreen` gets the screen's `dpr`, `canvasProps` to size a `<canvas>` in device pixels, and a `transform` to draw in canvas units; it draws again when the window moves to a screen of another density.
- b5558b8: PanZoomCanvas takes `min` and `max` for its zoom limits in percent (25 to 800 unless given), and ZoomControls takes the same `min` and `max`, offering only the presets between them.
- b5558b8: PanZoomCanvas takes `overlays`: controls in its four corners (start and end follow the page direction) that stay put while the content pans and zooms, keep their own pointer, wheel and keys, and never start a pan. The minimap now sits in the bottom-end corner under that corner's overlay.
- b5558b8: PathField: a new component for a file or folder path, a text field with a Browse button that asks your app's picker (`onPick(): Promise<string | null>`) and a clear button, with `value`/`onChange`, `name` and `form` for forms and `labels`. A picker that fails leaves the path as it was.
- b5558b8: Popover: `initialFocus` (`first`, `panel` or `none`, which leaves focus where it is so a hint can open beside a field being typed in) and `width` (`content`, `trigger` to be at least as wide as the trigger or anchor, or `fixed` for exactly the size preset). An anchor element now counts as inside, so pressing it no longer closes the panel.
- b5558b8: `useRovingFocus` takes `current: true` to treat the item marked `aria-current` as the selection, so the Tab stop starts on (and follows) the open entry of a list of links or buttons.
- b5558b8: RowActions: the buttons at the end of a row, hidden with a mouse until the row is hovered or holds focus or a menu of theirs is open, always shown on touch screens, and in the Tab order only on the row that holds the Tab stop. ListRow, Tree and ChatMessage render their actions with it, so they share one reveal rule (Tree's actions now fade by opacity like the others').
- b5558b8: New ScrollArea: a box that fills its parent and scrolls, with `direction`, `follow`, `onScroll`, `onAtBottomChange`, an `overlay` that stays put (its root is an OverlayFrame), and a `handle` with `scrollToEnd` and `scrollIntoView`. With a `label` it is a named region and a tab stop while there is something to scroll.
- b5558b8: SearchField: `trailing` shows status text inside the field's end, such as a match count ("3 of 12"), announced politely when it changes. New tokens `--cy-search-field-trailing-color` and `--cy-search-field-trailing-font-size`.
- b5558b8: One vocabulary and one look across lists and fields:
  - Select and TextArea now read the field skin (`--cy-field-control-border`, `--cy-field-control-bg`, `--cy-field-control-radius`, and for a read-only TextArea `--cy-field-control-sunken-bg`), so restyling fields once restyles every field's box.
  - VirtualList takes `selected` / `defaultSelected`, like Tree, Table and DataGrid. `selectedKeys` / `defaultSelectedKeys` still work but are deprecated.
  - Tabs reveals the selected tab with the shared `alignedScroll`. A padded tab wider than the strip now shows its start, as `scrollIntoView` does.
- b5558b8: SideNav works as an activity bar: `footer` pins entries (Settings, a notifications action) to the bottom, `placement="end"` puts the current indicator and the rail's tooltips on the side of a right-hand rail, and in the collapsed rail an item's badge sits on its icon's corner.
- b5558b8: Button, IconButton, Select, TextField, ProgressBar and SegmentedControl take `size="xs"` (the new `DenseSize` type): about 20px tall (the new global `--cy-control-height-xs`, `control.height.xs` in a theme), with smaller text, icons and padding, for dense desktop chrome. Button and TextField also take `--cy-button-height-xs`, `--cy-button-padding-inline-xs`, `--cy-text-field-height-xs` and `--cy-text-field-padding-inline-xs`. Only the look shrinks: the hit area stays at least 24px, and 44px on coarse pointers.
- b5558b8: New SortButton: the button in a sortable column's header, with the sort's icon. Table and DataGrid render it, so their sort headers share one look (Table's `cy-table-sort` and DataGrid's `cy-data-grid-sort` classes are gone).
- b5558b8: SplitPane: `collapsed`, `defaultCollapsed` and `onCollapsedChange` collapse a pane from a menu or button and restore its remembered size, `onResizeEnd` fires once when a drag or key resize ends, and `anchor="end"` gives the size to the end pane (a right sidebar or bottom dock that keeps its pixels). Collapsing no longer reports a size of 0 through `onSizeChange`: the size is kept.
- b5558b8: StatusBar and StatusBarSegment: the bar along the bottom of a desktop app, with start and end clusters of segments that show a tone, an icon or a status dot and monospace text, and as buttons open a Popover above them; arrow keys move between the clickable ones. `--cy-status-bar-soft-mix` sets the tint of soft segments, static or clickable.
- b5558b8: Tab: a new `closeLabel` prop names a closable tab's close button. Without it, a tab whose label is not a plain string (a badge, bold text) is named by the text it shows instead of its raw `value`.
- b5558b8: Tabs: `contextMenu` and `onCommand` on a Tab give it its own commands (Close others, Close to the right…) in a ContextMenu opened by right-click, a long press, Shift+F10 or the context menu key. A chosen command goes to `onCommand(value)`, the name Tree uses for its commands.
- b5558b8: Tabs: `dirty` on a Tab marks unsaved changes with a dot that turns into the close button on hover or focus; the close button's name and the tab's description say the changes are unsaved.
- b5558b8: Tabs: `trailing` on a TabList places content, such as a "New tab" button, right after the last tab and outside the tab list.
- b5558b8: Tabs: a label longer than `--cy-tabs-label-max-width` (default 20em) is cut short with an ellipsis, and the whole text shows in a tooltip on hover or focus.
- b5558b8: Tabs: a middle-click on a closable tab closes it, without starting the browser's autoscroll.
- b5558b8: Tabs: `scrollable` on a TabList adds chevrons at each end, shown only while there are more tabs that way, and scrolls the list sideways with a vertical mouse wheel.
- b5558b8: TagInput: Backspace in an empty field now takes the last tag back into the field to edit (announced through the new `labels.editing`) instead of only removing it. `renderTag(tag)` draws custom tag content, and `iconStart` puts an icon in the field.
- b5558b8: TextArea: mentions. `trigger` (such as "@") and `suggestions` (a list, or a function of the query returning items or a promise) open the shared drop-down list at the start of a word; arrows move, Enter or Tab inserts the value and a space, Escape closes it, and `onMention` reports the choice. The list is named by the field's label; new `labels.loading`.
- b5558b8: TextField: `boxRef` gives the field's box (the input with its icons, addons and buttons), so a list or popover can drop from the whole field. Combobox and TagInput anchor their lists with it.
- b5558b8: Text: `wrap="pre"` keeps spaces and line breaks as written, and `wrap="break"` breaks long ids and URLs anywhere instead of overflowing.
- b5558b8: TitleBar: the title bar of an installed app that draws its own (frameless Electron windows, Window Controls Overlay). The whole bar drags the window while the controls inside stay clickable, and it takes `leading`, `trailing`, `leadingInset`, an `overlay` mode, `onHeightChange` and window `controls` (minimize, maximize or restore, close).
- b5558b8: Toast: `toast({ id })` with the id of a showing toast updates it in place instead of adding another, `loading` shows a spinner and holds the toast until it is updated, and `onDismiss(id, reason)` says whether it timed out, the user closed it or `dismiss()` did.
- b5558b8: Tooltip: `disabled` turns the tooltip off without remounting its trigger, and `describe={false}` shows a tip that repeats the trigger's name without reading it to screen readers again; `whenTruncated` turns it on only while text is cut short (measured with one ResizeObserver) and implies `describe={false}`. Tabs, StatusBar and DataGrid use it, so StatusBar and DataGrid no longer read a cut-short text twice and DataGrid's tip shows only when the cell is cut.
- b5558b8: Tree, for schema explorers: items take `iconTone`/`iconColor`, `trailing`, a typed `data` payload, `actions` (buttons on hovered, focused or selected rows, in the Tab order after the focused row) and a `contextMenu` (one ContextMenu beside the tree), all reporting through `onCommand`; `kind: "group"` heading rows that count their children and are never selected; `filter` (a string or a function returning match positions) shows matches with their ancestors and marks them; `loadingIds`, `errorIds`, `onRetry`, `renderError` and `renderEmpty` for loading driven by a store; and a `handle` whose `reload(id)` runs `loadChildren` again.
- b5558b8: VirtualList takes `onAtBottomChange` and an `overlay` pinned over the bottom of the list (a "3 new" pill), and follows its end through the same helper as ScrollArea. The list now always sits in an OverlayFrame.

### Patch Changes

- b5558b8: Accordion: a controlled, collapsible single Accordion now closes its open section when `value` becomes undefined, instead of falling back to its own state and staying open.
- b5558b8: Select, Combobox, TagInput, TextArea's mentions and CommandPalette keep their active option the same way: kept while it is still in the list, else the first usable one, and always scrolled into view (TagInput's suggestions did not scroll before).
- b5558b8: Popovers, menus and drop-down lists flip to the other side when their content grows after opening and no longer fits.
- b5558b8: AppShell: the navigation drawer closes when the shell widens, so it no longer pops open by itself when the shell narrows again.
- b5558b8: Slider and NumberField write `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` without exponent notation (0.0000005, not 5e-7), as they already did `aria-valuenow`.
- b5558b8: Calendar no longer risks a hydration mismatch: the device's date, language and weekend settle in the browser before the first paint, and a server draws no day as today and uses `locale` (or English) and Monday-first weeks until then.
- b5558b8: Calendar builds its date formatters and reads each locale's week once instead of on every render (about 40 Intl objects per render before), so DateField stays light while typing.
- b5558b8: A Checkbox with `indeterminate` stays mixed after a click or Space while the prop is still true, instead of the browser clearing it.
- b5558b8: A controlled ColorField keeps its saturation and hue at black and through 8-bit rounding: bringing the brightness back up returns the colour you had, not grey. A `value` the parent changes itself is still read afresh.
- b5558b8: ColorSwatches no longer chooses a swatch when Tab lands on it (so a custom colour in ColorField survives tabbing through), and a click calls `onChange` once instead of twice.
- b5558b8: Combobox with `allowCustom` keeps the chosen option's value when you leave the field (it used to commit the label), and options that load after the field mounts now show the chosen label instead of blank text that cleared the value on blur.
- b5558b8: CompareSlider calls `onChange` only when the divider actually moves, not for Home at the start, arrows past an end or a pointer resting on one spot.
- b5558b8: `<CynosureTheme contrast="normal">` without a `theme` now keeps the normal palettes on an OS set to more contrast, instead of inheriting the high-contrast tokens from the root.
- b5558b8: DateField submits its date, not the text being typed: a form will not submit while the text is not an available date, and a form reset brings back `defaultValue`.
- b5558b8: DateField's calendar now opens on the chosen day inside a shadow root too; it used to find the calendar by id in the document, which missed it there.
- b5558b8: The FloatingToolbar and HoverCard offset tokens accept any length (`rem`, `calc()`), and the offset is read once when they open instead of on every render.
- b5558b8: Escape in a Menu or Popover opened from a FloatingToolbar or DockedToolbar now closes that menu first; the next Escape leaves the bar.
- b5558b8: `useHotkey` now matches Option shortcuts on macOS (`alt+k`) and shifted digits (`shift+1`) by the physical key, and accepts `+` as the key (`mod++`).
- b5558b8: TextField, Select and SegmentedControl at `size="lg"` show large (20px) icons, like a large Button, instead of medium ones, so a row of large controls shows one icon size.
- b5558b8: NumberField reads back its own text in every locale: other digits (Arabic-Indic in `ar-EG`, Devanagari…), currency text with dots such as "1.234,50 kr.", unit text and accounting brackets. Edits there are no longer undone on blur.
- b5558b8: ProgressBar: documents the slim bar in a row of text (`labelHidden` without `showValue`), with a story that checks it stays named, takes the space between its neighbours and fills from the reading start.
- b5558b8: Reduced motion no longer stops the animations of host elements whose class merely contains "cy-" (such as `.privacy-banner`); only Cynosure's own elements stop.
- b5558b8: A Menu or Popover inside a Toolbar keeps its own arrow keys, and the toolbar keeps its Tab stop while one is open.
- b5558b8: Escape in a SearchField while an input method is composing (Chinese, Japanese, Korean…) now only cancels the composition; it no longer clears the query or leaves the field.
- b5558b8: In an open Select, PageDown and PageUp stop on the last or first usable option when the end option is disabled, instead of losing the active option.
- b5558b8: Selected rows in Tree, VirtualList and DataGrid use the global `--cy-color-accent-soft`, the same tint as Table's selected rows (it was a slightly lighter mix of the accent written into each stylesheet).
- b5558b8: Inside a shadow root, closing a Popover, Menu or other layer returns focus to its trigger, a modal Popover keeps Tab inside it, and dismissing a focused Toast moves focus to the next toast instead of leaving the stack paused.
- b5558b8: SignaturePad draws again at the new density when the window moves to a screen of another pixel density (or the browser zooms), so the signature stays sharp; it measures through the same helper as PanZoomCanvas.
- b5558b8: SignaturePad draws faster: finished strokes keep their outline, the canvas is reallocated only when its size or pixel density changes, and the stroke in progress is drawn once per frame without re-rendering. What it draws and reports is unchanged.
- b5558b8: A controlled Slider `value` outside `min`/`max` now shows at the nearest end: the thumb, `aria-valuenow` and the arrow keys stay in range.
- b5558b8: Slow trackpad pinches now zoom DocumentViewport and PanZoomCanvas: Ctrl/Cmd+wheel steps under a whole percent add up instead of being rounded away.
- b5558b8: SortableList: a keyboard move is cancelled when focus leaves the handle, instead of staying picked up. Dropping applies the move to the items as they are now, so items the parent added during the move are kept.
- b5558b8: Tooltip, Combobox, TagInput and the components that float beside an anchor no longer log "useLayoutEffect does nothing on the server" when rendered on the server with React 18.
- b5558b8: Slider and NumberField round to the decimals of both `step` and `min`, so `min={0.5} step={1}` reaches 0.5, 1.5 and so on, and steps written as `1e-7` no longer round to 0. Their ARIA and form values are written without exponent notation.
- b5558b8: Table: selecting rows no longer drops selected ids that are not in the current `rows`, so a selection survives paging and filtering. The select-all acts on the rows shown.
- b5558b8: Tabs: tab values that differ only in punctuation, such as "a b" and "a_b", no longer share an element id, so each tab controls its own panel.
- b5558b8: TagInput's arrow keys between the input and the tags follow the reading direction, so in right-to-left text Arrow Right moves into the tags.
- b5558b8: TextArea fits its height again when its width changes (a resized window, a collapsing sidebar), so rewrapped text neither scrolls nor leaves empty lines.
- b5558b8: `CynosureTheme` no longer logs React 18's `useLayoutEffect` warning when rendered on the server.
- b5558b8: TimeField submits its 24-hour value, not the text being typed: a form will not submit while the text is not an allowed time, and a form reset brings back `defaultValue`.
- b5558b8: TimeField's arrow keys now keep the minutes on the `step` grid: with a step of 15, Up from 09:32 goes to 09:45 (not 09:47) and Down to 09:30.
- b5558b8: Swiping a Toast away now runs through the shared drag handling: Escape mid-swipe puts it back and taps on its buttons stay taps. A leaving toast (and a closing Dialog) waits for its longest exit transition, delays included.
- b5558b8: Tree: ArrowRight and ArrowLeft no longer move the tab stop onto a disabled row (nor does a selected disabled row take it), so the tree always stays in the Tab order. Expanded and selected rows are looked up through Sets, so large trees render faster.
- b5558b8: Type-ahead in Select, Menu, Tree and VirtualList now finds the first item when nothing is active yet, instead of skipping it.
- b5558b8: `"use client"` now marks only modules that run React, so `themeInitScript()`, `qrToSvg()` and the zoom and layout helpers can be called from React Server Components.
- b5558b8: VirtualList: moving rows with Alt+arrows or a drag is linear in the list's length (it was quadratic, so a large selection in a long list froze the page), and scrolling no longer re-keys every item or re-measures every wrapped row.

## 0.4.0

### Minor Changes

- 8f8a2df: New `@arshad-shah/cynosure/astro` adapter, an Astro integration that themes the page on the server: `cynosure({ theme })` adds the product theme as CSS on `:root` and Cynosure's stylesheet to every page, and the saved-mode script inline in `<head>` (`initScript`, default on); `htmlAttributes({ mode, contrast })` gives `<html>` its mode and contrast attributes. See `docs/adapters.md`.
- 8f8a2df: New `Choice`: the row a choice control sits in (the control, its label and description, the whole row clickable), with `strikeWhenChecked` and the tokens `--cy-choice-gap`, `--cy-choice-done-color` and `--cy-choice-strike-width`. Checkbox, Switch and RadioGroup's options are now Choice rows, and each owns its control's look in its own stylesheet: `styles/checkbox.css` holds only the box, the new `styles/switch.css` the switch, `styles/radio-group.css` the radio, and `styles/choice.css` the shared row (it used to be ChoiceBase's stylesheet, now `styles/choice-base.css`). Import Choice's stylesheet with Checkbox's, Switch's or RadioGroup's when you load stylesheets one by one (`styles/manifest.json` lists them). Props, tokens and the look are unchanged; `--cy-checkbox-done-color` and `--cy-checkbox-strike-width` now set the row's done tokens. `ChoiceControlProps` and `ChoiceInput` are exported for building new choice controls.
- 8f8a2df: New `DeviceFrame`: a phone, tablet or browser window drawn in CSS around a screenshot, a video or a live demo. The screen keeps its ratio at any width and clips its content to its corners, `orientation` turns a phone or tablet, and the browser bar shows an optional `url`. It is decoration by default and adds nothing to the accessibility tree; with `label` it is a named figure.
