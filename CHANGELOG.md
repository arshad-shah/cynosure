## 0.9.0

### Minor Changes

- ac8c759: Inline takes `inert` and `disabled` in its type (they already worked). List keeps an `aria-label` you pass, and Card an `aria-labelledby`. DocumentViewport reads its gap in any CSS length. CanvasStage's drop state ends when files leave over its overlay. CameraView reports a camera that stops while running (unplugged, access taken back) as the new error `lost` (`labels.lost`) and turns off. SplitPane's spoken value is overridable through the new `labels` (`percent`, `pixels`).
- ac8c759: 47 Facet icons (526 in all); the feed icon is the existing `rss` brand mark, each with search words in `ICON_KEYWORDS`:
  - "Off" states as the set draws them, the shape cut back around the slash: `bellOff`, `micOff`, `videoOff`, `cameraOff`, `cloudOff`, `pinOff`, `plugOff`, `phoneOff`, `flashlightOff`; and `filterX`.
  - Arrows: `chevronsLeft`, `chevronsRight`, `arrowLeftRight`, `arrowUpDown`.
  - People: `userMinus`, `userCheck`, `userX`, `circleUser`.
  - Tasks and time: `circleDashed` (to do), `circleProgress` (in progress), `kanban`, `listTodo`, `calendarCheck`, `calendarX`, `calendarClock`, `alarmClock`, `sunset` (beside `sunrise`).
  - Media: `audioWaveform`, `music`, `film`, `volume1`, `shuffle`, `pictureInPicture`, `captions`, `circlePause`, `monitorUp` (screen share, beside `monitorDown`).
  - Files and data: `cloudUpload`, `cloudDownload`, `fileAudio`, `fileVideo`.
  - Others: `phone`, `reply`, `mailOpen`, `fingerprint`, `flashlight`, `gamepad`, `aspectRatio`.

  `api` is also found by "webhook". The `icons/by-name` budget is 27.25 kB.
- ac8c759: Six Facet icons for image and key tools (479 in all): `flipHorizontal` and `flipVertical` (a shape and its mirror either side of a dashed axis; editing, flip), `pixelate` (a frame of uneven mosaic blocks, for redacting part of an image; editing, pop), `imageCode` (an image frame holding `< >`, for code to image; media, pop), `stepMarker` (a circled 1, the numbered callout dropped on screenshots; editing, pop) and `keyPair` (a padlock and a key, for public and private key generation; security, tilt). ### Patch Changes

- ac8c759: Data and lists: DataGrid no longer re-sorts and re-keys every row while scrolling when `sort` is controlled or `getRowId` is an inline function, and VirtualList no longer re-keys with an inline `getKey`. A hidden DataGrid column neither filters nor sorts (its filter and sort come back with it), and the keyboard tab stop stays on its row when rows change. Sorting compares dates by time, and booleans and bigints as values. DataGrid, Tree and VirtualList re-measure their rows when the row height changes (a new `size`). A Tree `reload()` answer that arrives after a newer one is ignored.
- ac8c759: Form fields: Enter in a Form now submits the value a NumberField, DateField, TimeField or ColorField has just committed (it sent the one before). DateField's arrows skip unavailable dates. A disabled Rating is left out of the form, and Rating and PathField follow a form reset. NumberField rejects text with characters it does not know (it read "12abc3" as 123) and reads every script's decimal digits, full-width ones included. NumberField, DateField, TimeField and ColorField leave Enter and the arrows to an input method while it composes. ColorField's invalid text blocks the form, as DateField's does. A RadioGroup without `name` submits nothing.
- ac8c759: Facet icons are drawn cleanly at every size: no stroke is drawn over another, a slash keeps 0.75 clear of what it crosses out, and strokes that do not meet no longer graze. Slash icons rebuilt to that rule: `bellOff`, `micOff`, `videoOff`, `cameraOff`, `cloudOff`, `pinOff`, `plugOff`, `phoneOff`, `flashlightOff`, `eyeOff`, `starOff`, `imageOff`, `wifiOff`, `powerOff`. Strokes no longer drawn twice in `bold`, `thumbsUp`, `thumbsDown`, `hand`, `gift`, `atSign`, `brush`, `caseLower`, `caseUpper`, `matchCase`, `circleUser`, `database`, `flower`, `gitMerge`, `hardDrive`, `listTree`, `scrollText` and `bookMarked`. Gaps opened in `shapes`, `bed`, `idCard`, `piggyBank`, `scissors`, `fileCompress`, `fileConvert`, `filePen` and `pageMerge`; `sunrise` and `sunset` rays clear the sun. `listTodo` (a tick and an open box), `fileAudio`, `fileVideo`, `alarmClock`, `gamepad`, `fingerprint`, `calendarClock`, `cloudUpload` and `cloudDownload` redrawn. New unit tests keep it so.
- ac8c759: Dialog and Popover open with focus on the first control Tab reaches, as their Tab trap already counts it: a radio group's checked radio, never an unchecked one or a roving item that is not the group's stop. Tabs read their reveal padding, and DocumentViewport its gap, in any CSS length, as Popover's offset already was.
- ac8c759: Overlays and focus: a modal Popover's Tab trap no longer lets focus out past a roving group or a radio group at its end. CommandPalette opened from a menu item returns focus to the menu's button. Dialog, Sheet, ProgressOverlay and CommandPalette render open in jsdom (no dialog API). A Dialog that cannot be dismissed stays open when the browser closes it. A hidden HoverCard is inert on React 18 too. Escape on a TagInput tag no longer also closes the Dialog around it. Roving focus finds the focused item inside shadow roots.
- ac8c759: Theming: `themeToCss(theme, ":host")` writes `:host([data-cy-mode="dark"])`, which matches (`:host[…]` never did), and a custom selector without a contrast attribute follows the OS contrast setting as the default one does. Theme values with an unclosed quote or bracket, `url(`, `image(`, `image-set(` or `@` are rejected, so a plugin theme cannot break the stylesheet or fetch a URL. `hsl()` reads negative hues, hue units (`deg`, `grad`, `rad`, `turn`) and modern syntax without `%`, and an unknown unit is an error instead of another colour. `iconToSvg` escapes every attribute. `cy check` also catches native elements created with `createElement` or `jsx`, through a constant tag name, and `title` in spread props.
- ac8c759: Text's `size3xl`, `size4xl` and `size5xl` theme tokens now reach the stylesheet: `createTheme({ components: { text: { size3xl } } })` wrote `--cy-text-size3xl`, while Text reads `--cy-text-size-3xl`. A number after a letter in a component token's name now starts a word, as in the global `--cy-font-size-3xl`. Toolbar (`gap`, `padding`), Accordion (`gap`) and Icon (`size`, `sizeSm`, `sizeMd`, `sizeLg`, `stroke`) tokens are typed, as are Checkbox `sizeSm` and `sizeLg` and Switch `heightSm` and `heightLg`; a unit test fails when a stylesheet reads a component token its type does not declare.

- a77ef74: ColorSample (new): a colour shown as a surface, or text in one colour (`ink`) on another with its WCAG contrast ratio, cut to two decimals, and AA and AAA verdicts (`largeText` for the large-text thresholds). - a77ef74: ColorSwatches: `readOnly` shows the colours without offering a choice, as a group of named colour images with no tab stop and no tick; `onChange` is optional.
- a77ef74: CompareSlider: both sides share one box, the shape of the first picture, video or canvas, and any child is laid out in it: pictures, videos and canvases are contained and centred, so two of different sizes or shapes line up, and other children (an Image with the same `ratio`) are stretched to it. `beforeSrc` / `afterSrc` with `beforeAlt` / `afterAlt` draw the two pictures without writing an `img`.
- a77ef74: CompareSlider zooms: `zoom` / `defaultZoom` / `onZoomChange` take DocumentViewport's `ZoomSetting` (fit page by default, fit width, or a percent of the picture's own size, 100 being actual size), so ZoomControls drives it with `onScaleChange`'s scale. Both sides scale together in a frame that keeps its size; larger than the frame they pan (drag the picture, the wheel, or the zoomed view's arrows as a tab stop), and the divider still moves by its line. `zoomable` adds Ctrl/Cmd+wheel, pinch and Ctrl/Cmd = - 0. New labels `view` and `zoom(percent)`; a new setting is announced.

  Columns take `label`, their plain-text name for the editor, filter and menu. New labels and token `--cy-data-grid-details-background`.
- a77ef74: FileDrop: `variant="button"` draws only the picker button (with the upload icon), a file-picker button for one action among others. Nothing is dropped on it; `accept`, `multiple`, the size and count checks, `onFiles`, `onPick`, forms and announcements work as in a zone, and refused files are listed under it.
- a77ef74: HtmlPreview (new): untrusted HTML (an email, an HTTP response) in a frame with every sandbox restriction and a content security policy (`htmlPolicy`), so no script runs and nothing loads but embedded pictures. Remote images are an opt-in (`remoteImages`, `onRemoteImagesChange`), offered by a Notice when the HTML refers to one. FrameBase (new foundation) is the one place an `<iframe>` is rendered, sandboxed by default; `cy check` now keeps `<iframe>` and `<video>` to foundations.
- a77ef74: Image: `overlay` draws content over the picture's own box (inside a `contain` picture's letterbox, the whole cropped picture with `cover`), measured again as the frame resizes so it scales with the picture; for a safe-zone ring, crop marks or a grid. - a77ef74: Select `emptyOption` ("Any", "None"): a first option, before any groups, whose value is `""`, so a filter needs no sentinel value. - a77ef74: SelectionFrame keeps a small object's body free of handles: below `compactBelow` on screen (twice the hit area by default: 48px, 88px on touch screens), the handles across the short side are hidden and the rest move just outside the object, so a tick or a line of highlight can still be dragged by its body and resized by its corners. - a77ef74: Slider commits keyboard changes when the key is released, not on every key press: a held arrow key repeats `onChange` and calls `onCommit` once, and a key that changes nothing commits nothing. `commitDelay` (ms) waits that long after the last key, so several presses run a costly `onCommit` once; leaving the slider commits a pending change at once.
- a77ef74: SortButton `rank`: the column's place among several sort keys, shown small after the icon (DataGrid's multi-column sort). Its `onClick` now receives the click event, so a Shift+click can be told apart.
- a77ef74: Table: a column's `tone` tints its header and cells, and `tone: (row) => Tone | undefined` tints cell by cell for a heat grid; in markup, `TableCell` and `TableHeaderCell` take `tone`. The tone is mixed into the surface (`--cy-table-tint-mix`, 14%) with the text kept in the ink colour.
- a77ef74: TextArea: `onFiles` takes files dropped on the field, filtered by `accept`; while files are over it the field shows the accent border and fill, and the drop leaves the text alone so the caller can read a text file (`await file.text()`) and set the value. - a77ef74: VirtualList `onRangeChange(start, end)`: the rows in view (end exclusive) whenever they change, on mount, scroll and resize, never twice for the same rows, for rows fetched by position (a log held in a worker). Rows that expand are measured again with `wrap` (documented and tested).
- a77ef74: ZoomControls works for a canvas as well as a document: `fits` chooses the menu's fit modes (`[]` for none), `buttons` adds Fit to view (`fit-page`, the whole content) and Actual size (100%), `steps` sets the percents zoom in and out go through, and `zoom` is optional (the preset equal to `percent` is marked). ### Patch Changes

- a77ef74: Card: a header's `actions` move under the title when the two no longer fit on one row, instead of squeezing the title to a sliver on a phone. The title keeps at least `--cy-card-title-min` (12rem, new token `card.titleMin`) before the actions wrap, and a long row of actions wraps within the card.
- a77ef74: Combobox: a controlled `value` shows its option's label in the input from the first render, and a value set from outside replaces the text (or clears it) unless the person is typing, without the product keeping `inputValue`.
- a77ef74: A pressed solid Button (and IconButton) keeps its solid fill instead of the tone's pale tint, so its text stays readable: before, the on-tone text sat on the tint (white on a light tint, or black on a dark one) and BitField's set bits could not be read. Pressed soft, outline and ghost buttons take the tone's text colour on the tint.
- a77ef74: SelectionFrame: dragging the object's body moves it again with a real mouse, pen or finger. The body's drag read the pointer button from the move that started it, which browsers report as -1, so it gave up at the first move; it now goes through `useDrag`.
- 7ba7723: TimeField: a press in the picker chooses only the pressed row. When the column had just moved (the browser bringing the row into view), the press used to choose the allowed time nearest to where the column stopped. A press on an unavailable hour now chooses nothing, and the column goes back to the current time.
- a77ef74: Tooltip (and so IconButton's label tip, Dialog's and Sheet's close button) no longer opens on focus a script moves: opening a Dialog or Sheet focused its close button for a moment and left the "Close" tip up, so the first Escape closed the tip instead of the dialog. A tip now opens on focus only when Tab, the arrows, Home, End or Page Up/Down moved it there and the trigger still has focus a frame later, and closes as soon as focus leaves the trigger by any route (a script's `focus()` included) unless the pointer rests on it. One Escape closes a Dialog, Sheet or Popover right after it opens.

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 953afff: `AppShell` now shares its narrow state, so products no longer repeat the breakpoint. `useAppShell()` returns `{ narrow }` to anything rendered inside the shell (it is `false` on the server, on the first client render and outside a shell), and the new `onNarrowChange` prop tells code outside the shell once per change.
- 953afff: New `AspectRatio` component: a box that keeps a width-to-height ratio at any width, for a video, a map, a chart or an embed. `ratio` takes a number or a CSS ratio such as `"4/3"` (default `16 / 9`) and the height comes from CSS, so nothing is measured. Its children are stretched to the box, images and videos cover it, and `radius` rounds and clips the corners from the radius tokens.
- 953afff: New `Avatar`: a person, team or thing as a picture, falling back to initials worked out from the name (emoji and non-Latin names included) and then to an icon. It has five sizes, circle and square shapes, tones (with `tone="auto"` picking one from the name), a status dot that is spoken with the name, and `decorative` for when the name is written beside it.

  New `AvatarGroup`: overlaps several avatars along the reading direction, gives them one size and sums up the rest as a "+3" avatar that names the hidden people.
- 953afff: New `Breadcrumbs` component: the trail from the top of a site or app to the current page, as a "Breadcrumb" navigation landmark with an ordered list. Pass `items` (`label`, `href`, `onClick`, `icon`); every item but the last is a link or a button, and the last is the current page (`aria-current="page"`). `maxItems` collapses the middle of a long trail into a menu, long names are cut with an ellipsis and the trail wraps in narrow containers.
- 953afff: Button and IconButton now set `aria-current` on the element in both forms, so a link-form button (`href`) can say it is the current page; a link-form button also takes `style` now. Pagination with `getHref` renders the current page as a link with `aria-current="page"` instead of a button. Pagination's `onChange` gets the click event as a second argument, `(page, event)`, so a client-side router can call `event.preventDefault()` on a link press and navigate itself.
- 953afff: `Calendar` gains `markDay`, which marks a day without drawing it: a `tone` tints the cell, `dots` puts up to three coloured dots under the number, and `label` is added to the day's accessible name. The day state passed to `renderDay` and `markDay` has a `weekend` flag that follows the locale. With `renderDay`, a day's height is its own token, `--cy-calendar-content-size`, and defaults to 66px on touch screens (it was 110px), so a six-week month fits a phone while every day stays a 44px target.
- 953afff: `Calendar` can have its month set from outside: `month` (controlled, `YYYY-MM` or any date of the month) and `defaultMonth`, with `onMonthChange` reporting the month asked for. `monthPicker` turns the month heading into a button that opens a panel to jump to any month and year within `min` and `max`. The focused day now always stays inside the month shown.
- 953afff: `Card` takes `edge`: a coloured edge on its inline-start side for a category or status. A tone name uses that tone's colour and any other string is a CSS colour. It follows the reading direction and the card's radius, works with every variant and on link and button cards, and its thickness is the new token `--cy-card-edge-width`. Pair it with text or a Badge, since colour alone carries no meaning for many people.
- 953afff: `Checkbox` has a checklist style: with `strikeWhenChecked`, a checked box strikes through and mutes its label and mutes the description, so a finished task reads as done. The muted label keeps 4.5:1 contrast in light and dark, the strike stays in forced colours, and the new tokens `--cy-checkbox-done-color` and `--cy-checkbox-strike-width` adjust it. Switch and RadioGroup are unchanged.
- 953afff: New `Checklist`: a named list of rows people add, tick, rename in place and remove, such as a task's subtasks. It works controlled (`items`) or uncontrolled (`defaultItems`), moves focus to a sensible control after every change, and has `maxItems`, `reorderable` (drag or keyboard, through SortableList), `disabled`, `readOnly`, two sizes and overridable labels.
- 953afff: New `Container`: the page column. It centres its content, caps its width (`size`: `sm` 640px, `md` 768px, `lg` 1024px by default, `xl` 1280px, or `full` for no cap) and adds side gutters from a space token that never get narrower than a phone's safe area. `paddingBlock`, `center` and `as` (`div`, `main`, `section`, `article`) cover the rest, and the widths are tokens.
- 953afff: The default changed: `Dialog` and `Sheet` no longer keep their content mounted while closed; pass the new `keepMounted` prop to keep the old behaviour. The content now mounts when the dialog opens and unmounts once its closing transition has ended (at once under reduced motion), so every opening starts with fresh state, a closed dialog renders an empty `<dialog>` element (on the server too), and tests can no longer find the content of a closed dialog. Use `keepMounted` for content whose state must survive closing or that is expensive to rebuild. This also applies to what is built on them: `ProgressOverlay`, and the navigation drawer of `AppShell`.
- 953afff: New `Divider`: a line that separates content. It is horizontal by default and fills its container; `orientation="vertical"` stretches it to the height of a row such as an Inline or a footer of links. It can carry a short label in the line (`labelAlign` places it), be `dashed`, take a `strong` tone and add its own `spacing`. It is decorative by default; `decorative={false}` makes it a `role="separator"` named by its label.
- 953afff: `Grid` `columns` also takes a count per breakpoint, `columns={{ base: 1, sm: 2, lg: 4 }}`. The breakpoints (`sm` 30rem, `md` 45rem, `lg` 60rem, `xl` 75rem, exported as `GRID_BREAKPOINTS`) are widths of the grid itself, not the window, so it adapts inside panels, popups and shadow roots. It is CSS only, so server-rendered markup is right on the first paint, and `GridItem` spans are clamped to the columns in effect at each width.
- 953afff: New `Heading` component: a section or page heading whose `level` (the `h1` to `h6` element) is separate from its `size` (`xs` to `2xl`, plus `display` for a hero title), so the page outline stays correct whatever the heading looks like. It has `tone`, `weight`, `align` and `truncate` (one line or a number of lines), uses the display font with a tight line height and balanced wrapping, and has no margins. Each size has a token (`--cy-heading-size-*`) so a product can retune its heading scale.
- b4dc7bf: Icons: `ICON_KEYWORDS` (from `@arshad-shah/cynosure/icons`) maps each Facet icon's name to other words it is found by (`trash`: delete, remove, bin), for galleries and icon pickers. The words are a separate export, so importing an icon does not bring them along.
- 953afff: New `Image` component: a picture that never shifts the layout. `ratio` reserves its space with AspectRatio (or `width` and `height` do), a Skeleton shows while it loads, and a quiet box with the image icon and the `alt` text takes its place when it fails; `placeholder` and `fallback` replace either. It also has `fit`, `position`, `radius`, lazy loading by default, a `caption` that makes a named `figure`, and it notices a picture that had already loaded before React attached.
- 953afff: `Stack`, `Inline` and `Grid` take `padding`, `paddingInline` and `paddingBlock`: space tokens that pad the layout's own box, so a wrapper element is no longer needed for it. The specific prop wins over `padding`, and a nested layout never inherits its parent's padding. Layouts that do not use the new props are unchanged.
- 953afff: Icons in options can be coloured. `Select` and `Combobox` options and `Menu` and `ContextMenu` items take `iconTone` and `iconColor`, with the meaning of Icon's `tone` and `color`. Select keeps the colour in its trigger once the option is chosen. Disabled options show the icon muted, and icons without the new fields look as before.
- 953afff: New `PageHeader` component: the top of a page or a major section, with `breadcrumbs`, an `eyebrow`, the `title` (the header's one heading, at `headingLevel` 1 to 3), a lead `description`, a `meta` row, `actions` and content below. It is built from `Heading` and `Text`. The actions sit at the inline end and move below the text when the header's own width is under 36rem; `size="lg"` with `align="center"` makes a landing page hero, and `divider` draws a bottom border.
- 953afff: New component: Pagination, for moving between the pages of a list or table. It is a navigation landmark of Buttons with previous and next, numbered pages with ellipses, optional first and last controls, a compact "Page 3 of 10" variant for phones, and real links through `getHref`.

  The helpers `pageCount(total, pageSize)` and `pageRange(page, count, siblings, boundaries)` are exported with it.
- 953afff: `SideNav` takes one primary action among its items: `{ kind: "action", id, label, icon, onClick }` (type `NavAction`). In `layout="bar"` it is a round accent button raised above the tab bar, placed where it sits in `items`; in the column it is a full-width accent button and in the rail an icon button. It is a button, never the current page, and does not call `onNavigate`. New tokens: `--cy-side-nav-action-size` and `--cy-side-nav-action-lift`.
- 953afff: New `Text` component: one piece of text in the type scale, for labels, captions, lead paragraphs, helper lines and values. It has presets (`body`, `lead`, `caption`, `eyebrow`, `code`), `size`, `weight`, `tone` and `align` props that map to the font and colour tokens, one-line or multi-line `truncate`, and `numeric` for tabular figures. It renders a `span` by default or the element given in `as`, adds no margins, and is not changed by host CSS.
- b4dc7bf: TimeField can have a picker, and shows the time the way the person's region writes it.
  - `picker` adds a clock button at the end of the field that opens columns rolling under one lens, with a strip of the whole day to jump along (`dayTrack={false}` hides it) and the hours outside `min` and `max` hatched. It is off by default: fields that do not ask for it look as they did.
  - `hourCycle` is `"auto"` by default: `9:30 PM` where AM and PM are used, `21:30` elsewhere, from the browser or from `locale`. `hourCycle={24}` keeps the form every TimeField had before; `hourCycle={12}` fixes the 12-hour form and adds an AM/PM switch to the picker. The value, the limits and what a form receives stay 24-hour `HH:MM`.
  - New labels (`open`, `hours`, `minutes`, `seconds`, `am`, `pm`, `period`, `dayTrack`, `now`) and tokens (`--cy-time-field-row-height`, `-column-width`, `-lens-background`, `-night`, `-day`).
- 953afff: `Toaster` takes `offset`, to keep toasts clear of fixed parts of an app such as a bottom tab bar on a phone. One value (a space step like `"8"` or a CSS length like `"56px"`) moves them in from the top or bottom edge they sit on; `{ block, inline }` also sets the distance from the side. The same distances are the new tokens `--cy-toast-offset-block` and `--cy-toast-offset-inline`, so an app can set them in CSS, for example only below a breakpoint. The toast region now also stays inside the device's safe area (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`) on every side.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 98117b8: SegmentedControl: two controls given the same `name` no longer share one selection.
