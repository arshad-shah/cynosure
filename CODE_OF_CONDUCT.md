# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not on people.
- Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: this repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, the docs site, and any official community channels.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer: **arshad@arshadshah.com**.
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour violates this
policy.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is the sole enforcement authority. Decisions are final for
this project; external parties may of course fork under the MIT licence.
